# BOSCO Today — Management proposal

[Open the presentation](https://luka9333.github.io/Bosco/management/)

12 slides covering guest access, QR codes and social media, sales opportunities, daily ownership and costs, and a measurable seven-day pilot.

Use Next/Previous or arrow keys to turn pages. Press F for fullscreen. The slide selector jumps to a topic. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

The browser presentation is self-contained and works offline after saving its HTML. Keep the PDF and PowerPoint beside it for the download buttons to work offline. The PDF is a static 12-page copy with selectable text. The PowerPoint is a static visual backup with slide images and speaker notes; it has no editable layout or page-turn animation.

The QR code links to the current guest guide prototype. Campaigns and offers are proposals. No sales uplift, operating prices or analytics results are claimed. QR signs, campaign redemption, analytics and the recommended phone navigation need operational setup.

The existing guest guide remains at https://luka9333.github.io/Bosco/book/ .
